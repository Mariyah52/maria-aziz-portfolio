A WhatsApp assistant for furniture and interior businesses

Furniture buyers message several showrooms at once, usually in the evening, asking for photos, sizes and prices. IMADI Tech sets up an AI assistant on your WhatsApp Business number that answers them instantly with your designs and price ranges, asks for room size and city, sends a quotation, and follows up if they go quiet.

The problem

Where leads get lost

Price questions at night

Most "price?" and "do you have this in walnut?" messages arrive after the showroom closes and wait until the next day.

Quotes that go quiet

Custom sofas, beds and wardrobes need a quote, and many buyers disappear after receiving one without a follow-up.

Leads on salespeople's phones

When each salesperson handles their own chats, the owner can't see which leads were answered, quoted or lost.

What it does

What your WhatsApp assistant handles

  • Shares your catalog photos and price ranges for sofas, beds, dining sets and wardrobes
  • Asks the questions your team needs: product, size, fabric or wood, city and budget
  • Sends a PDF quotation on WhatsApp with your logo
  • Follows up the next day, and again a few days later, with buyers who stopped replying
  • Hands serious buyers to your salesperson with the full chat attached
  • Shows every lead, its status and its salesperson in one dashboard

FAQ

Common questions

Can the assistant show my furniture designs?

Yes. It shares photos and price ranges from a catalog and price sheet that you update yourself, so new designs and price changes go live without calling a developer.

Can it handle custom sizes and fabrics?

Yes. It collects the size, fabric or wood, color and city, then either sends a quotation from your price list or hands the chat to your team for a custom quote.

Will my salespeople still talk to customers?

Yes. The assistant handles first replies and common questions, then passes buyers who are ready to your salespeople, with the full conversation attached.
