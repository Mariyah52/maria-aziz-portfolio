The problem

Why businesses lose leads they already paid for

Late replies

A customer asks for a price at 10pm and gets an answer the next afternoon. By then they have messaged two other shops.

No follow-up

After a quotation, many customers go quiet. Nobody has time to follow up with each one, so the sale slips away.

Leads spread across phones

Enquiries sit on several salespeople's phones, so the owner cannot see which leads were answered, quoted, won or lost.

More ad spend, same sales

When enquiries are not handled well, raising the ad budget brings more messages but not more orders.

What's included

What the WhatsApp lead system does

  • Instant replies on WhatsApp in English or Roman Urdu, set up around your products, prices and questions
  • Shares your catalogue, designs and price ranges from a sheet you can update yourself
  • Asks qualifying questions such as product, size, city and budget
  • Sends quotations as PDF documents on WhatsApp
  • Automatic follow-up messages for customers who stop replying
  • Hands the chat to your salesperson when a customer asks for a person or is ready to buy
  • A dashboard with every lead and its status: new, in process, quoted, won or lost
  • Instagram DM and comment replies, connected to the same lead list (add-on)

How it works

From first call to live system

  1. Free 10-minute demo. See the assistant reply, ask questions and send a quotation, using an example like your own business.
  2. Workflow call. We map your products, price list, common questions and who on your team handles which leads.
  3. WhatsApp Business setup. We connect your number through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform and complete Meta's verification steps with you.
  4. Build and test. IMADI Tech builds the conversation, quotation and follow-up flows and tests them with you before customers see them.
  5. Go live and review. Your team gets the dashboard, and we review the results with you and adjust the flows.
