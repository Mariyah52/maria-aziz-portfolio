Farmaz'ona Parlour Dashboard

Live Farmaz'ona, beauty parlour · Karachi, Pakistan Nov 2026 – Present
In short

ProblemA beauty parlour needed billing, client records, stock and the daily closing in one place, reachable from a phone.

SolutionA dashboard that bills against a 94-service list, sends the bill on WhatsApp, tracks clients and stock, and closes the day for the owner.

The Problem

A beauty parlour needed billing, client records, stock and the daily closing in one place its staff could reach from a phone, with the bill going straight to the customer.

The Challenge

A parlour runs on a long price list and a small team on their feet, so the system had to handle 94 services, several staff logins and the day's closing without anyone sitting at a computer.

The Solution

A dashboard that bills a client in a few taps, sends the bill on WhatsApp, keeps client history and inventory, and closes the day with a summary the owner can check from anywhere.

Key Features

  • Billing against a 94-service rate list
  • Bills sent to the customer on WhatsApp
  • Client records and visit history
  • Inventory and stock tracking
  • Separate staff logins
  • Daily closing and analysis for the owner

My Role

Founder & AI Specialist. Designed and built the whole system, deployed it, and moved the database to cut page times after the client's testing round.

Technology

PythonFlaskPostgreSQLRenderWhatsApp

Architecture

Flask applicationPostgreSQL databaseStaff authenticationWhatsApp bill deliveryOwner analytics and daily closing

What's Different

Built phone-first, because parlour staff are standing with a client in front of them rather than sitting at a desk.

Results

  • Live for the client after a full testing round
  • Page times cut from about a second to roughly 300ms by moving the database into the same region as the app
  • 94-service rate list in use
  • Phase 2 agreed with the client: a WhatsApp booking bot
