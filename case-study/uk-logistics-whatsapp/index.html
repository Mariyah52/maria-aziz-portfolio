WhatsApp Automation & Operations Platform

Live UK Logistics · United Kingdom (returning client) Sep 2026 – Present
In short

ProblemAs a B2B company, staff had to sort through emails to find and handle damage claims.

SolutionA WhatsApp bot now runs the whole claims process, and staff raise, approve or reject claims from one dashboard. It saves time and keeps all data in sync.

The Problem

Rate quotes, order enquiries and damage claims were handled manually, so claims were hard to track and customers waited for quotes.

The Solution

A WhatsApp-first system on Meta's Cloud API. Customers get instant pallet-rate quotes and place order enquiries; registered corporate clients file damage claims step by step with photos and invoices, and administrators review and approve them from one dashboard.

Key Features

  • Intent routing that recognises registered corporate senders
  • Instant pallet-rate quotes pulled live from a Google Sheets rate card
  • Order enquiry capture
  • Multi-step claims flow: shipment details, labelled photo evidence and proof-of-cost invoices
  • Claims approval workflow with automatic WhatsApp notifications
  • Operations dashboard: orders, claims review, evidence, company administration and role-based admin access

My Role

Founder & AI Specialist. Designed the conversation flows and architecture and built the bot and dashboard.

Technology

Architecture

What's Different

One WhatsApp number serves two audiences: it recognises registered companies automatically, and every claim runs from first message to final decision with its evidence attached. Built for reliability with persistent conversation state, idempotent webhook processing, secure media handling and audit logging.
