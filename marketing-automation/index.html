FAQ

Common questions

Does the bot post by itself?

Yes. Once it's set up, it designs and publishes posts, carousels and reels to Instagram and Facebook on your schedule without anyone logging in.

Is it safe for my Instagram account?

Yes. It publishes through Meta's official Graph API using your own business account, not through unofficial apps or shared logins.

Can it make reels with voice?

Yes. Reels can include a natural English voiceover, text scenes in your brand style, and screen recordings of your product or work.

Can it post to WhatsApp Status?

No. WhatsApp doesn't allow automatic Status posting for business numbers, so the bot focuses on Instagram and Facebook and adds your WhatsApp link to every post.
