How much does a WhatsApp chatbot cost in Pakistan?

By Maria Aziz, founder of IMADI Tech

Short answer: a WhatsApp chatbot has three costs. A one-time setup fee to build it, a monthly fee for hosting and support, and Meta's own WhatsApp charges, which Meta bills to your business directly. How much each one comes to depends mostly on how many conversations you have and how much the bot needs to do.

1. The setup (one time)

This is the work of building the assistant around your business. It usually covers:

  • Mapping your products, price list and the questions customers ask every day
  • Writing the conversation: greetings, qualifying questions, hand-off to your team
  • Connecting your number to Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform and completing Meta's verification
  • Extras such as PDF quotations, automatic follow-ups or a lead dashboard

A simple bot that answers common questions costs far less than one that sends quotations, follows up and feeds a dashboard. That's why any honest quote comes after a short call about your workflow.

2. Hosting and support (monthly)

The bot runs on a server around the clock. The monthly fee covers keeping it online, updating answers and prices, fixing anything that breaks, and adjusting the flows as you learn what customers ask.

3. Meta's WhatsApp charges (paid to Meta)

Meta charges per delivered message, depending on the message category and the customer's country. Pakistan has its own rate on Meta's rate card. In broad terms, as of October 2026:

  • Messages from customers are free. You're never charged for what customers send you.
  • Service replies: each business number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, then they're charged.
  • Marketing, utility and authentication templates (for example, a follow-up sent after 24 hours, or an order update) are charged per message at Meta's published rates.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ads: when someone messages you from one of these ads and you reply within 24 hours, Meta opens a free entry point window (up to 7 days) for your messages to that customer.

Meta updates these rules regularly, so check Meta's WhatsApp pricing page for the latest rates. For most small businesses, Meta's charges are small next to the value of the sales a fast reply wins back.

WhatsApp Business app vs a chatbot

The free WhatsApp Business app is fine for a handful of chats a day, with quick replies and an away message. Once you get many inquiries from ads, or need quotations, follow-ups and more than one person answering, a chatbot on the WhatsApp Business Platform starts to pay for itself.

How to keep the cost down

  • Start with the one flow that loses you the most sales, usually first replies and follow-ups
  • Keep prices in a sheet you update yourself, so changes don't need a developer
  • Answer inside the 24-hour window, where replies to customers cost less
  • Add quotations and a dashboard once the basics are working
