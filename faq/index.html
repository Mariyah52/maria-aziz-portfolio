IMADI Tech · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about WhatsApp chatbots, lead automation and working with IMADI Tech.

What does IMADI Tech do?

IMADI Tech builds AI automation for businesses: WhatsApp chatbots and lead follow-up systems, custom ERP and business management software, and SaaS products. It was founded in May 2026 by Maria Aziz in Karachi, Pakistan, and has built platforms for clients in Pakistan, the UK and Kenya.

Who is IMADI Tech for?

IMADI Tech works with small and mid-sized, owner-led businesses that get many enquiries on WhatsApp, Instagram or Facebook. It is a strong fit for furniture, kitchen, interior, building supply and logistics businesses, where each order needs a quotation.

How does a WhatsApp chatbot help a business get more sales?

A WhatsApp chatbot replies to every enquiry within seconds, so customers get an answer while they are still interested. It also asks qualifying questions, sends quotations and follows up with people who go quiet, which are the steps where most leads are usually lost.

Does the chatbot replace my sales team?

No. The chatbot handles first replies, common questions, quotations and reminders, then hands the conversation to your salesperson when a customer asks for a person or is ready to buy. Your team spends its time on serious buyers.

Will I lose my WhatsApp number or my old chats?

You keep your business number, but it is connected to Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform. Before setup, IMADI Tech checks with you whether your number can stay on the WhatsApp Business app alongside the platform or whether a separate number is better, so you know exactly what happens to your existing chats.

Can the chatbot reply in Roman Urdu?

Yes. The assistant can be set up to reply in English or Roman Urdu, matching how your customers write to you.

Can it send quotations and price lists?

Yes. The assistant can share your catalogue and price ranges from a sheet you update yourself, and send quotations as PDF documents on WhatsApp.

Can IMADI Tech automate Instagram messages too?

Yes, as an add-on. Instagram direct messages and replies to comments can be answered automatically and added to the same lead list as WhatsApp. This needs an Instagram professional account linked to your Facebook Page.

How long does setup take?

Setup time depends on how many products and flows you need, and Meta's business verification can add time. You get a timeline in writing before work starts.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is a one-time setup fee plus monthly support, quoted after a free 10-minute demo once your products and flows are clear. Meta bills its WhatsApp messaging charges directly to your business.

Where is IMADI Tech based, and do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

IMADI Tech is based in Karachi, Pakistan, and works remotely with clients elsewhere. It has delivered projects for businesses in Pakistan, the United Kingdom and Kenya, and also takes on clients in the UAE.
