ProblemAs a B2B company, staff had to sort through emails to find and handle damage claims.

Company Monitor

Multi-branch business · Nairobi, Kenya

ProblemSalesmen were taking leads from WhatsApp, delivering goods from outside the company and keeping the commission.

SolutionEach branch scans a QR code to connect its WhatsApp to a dashboard, so management sees every lead and conversation for every branch and salesman.

ReNaz: WhatsApp Scrap Collection Platform

Scrap collection business · Pakistan

ProblemThe client needed data on how much scrap comes from across Karachi, an easy way to collect it, and verified collectors who can enter gated communities.

SolutionCustomers and collectors do everything through a WhatsApp conversation, and the data reaches a dashboard automatically. Collectors register with their CNIC. 23+ pickups completed.
